Simple steps

How CUAgain works

From creating a report to contacting another student, the process is designed to be straightforward.

01

Submit a report

Choose whether you lost or found an item. Add its name, category, location, date, description, and an optional photo.

02

Browse and connect

Search the item board and open a report for more details. Use Request to Chat or Community Chat to contact other students.

03

Arrange a safe return

Confirm that the item belongs to the claimant and coordinate a suitable campus handover.

Keep the community helpful

Community guidelines

Follow these reminders when posting reports, contacting other students, or arranging item returns.

1

Provide accurate information

Use a clear item name and description. Include the date and general campus location where it was lost or found.

2

Protect personal information

Do not post passwords, student numbers, phone numbers, home addresses, or other sensitive details publicly.

3

Verify ownership

Before returning an item, ask the claimant to describe identifying details that are not fully disclosed in the public report.

4

Communicate respectfully

Use Community Chat for public discussions and Messages for private conversations. Do not harass, spam, or impersonate others.

5

Arrange handovers carefully

Choose a visible, appropriate campus location and, when possible, coordinate during school hours or with campus personnel.

6

Be honest and update reports

Do not submit false reports or claim items that are not yours. Update or close a report once the item has been returned.

Helpful information

Frequently asked questions

Can I report both lost and found items?

Yes. In the student portal, choose the report type that matches your situation and complete the item details.

How do I contact the person who posted?

Open the item details and select Request to Chat. You can also select a participant in Community Chat to start a private conversation.

Can I hide my identity when reporting a found item?

The report form includes an option to hide the finder's identity from the public item details in this prototype.

Is this connected to a live student account system?

Not yet. This current version is a frontend demonstration. Reports, accounts, and messages are sample/local data and are not shared with other users.
